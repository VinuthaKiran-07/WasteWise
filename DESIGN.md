# WasteWise visual direction

WasteWise is an operate-mode product for cafeteria managers. The screen should feel like a dependable daily planning desk: easy to scan, compact enough for repeat use, and expressive only where it improves a decision.

## World

The visual system borrows from service schedules and food-service production boards: clear column rhythm, strong numeric hierarchy, and status colors that explain operational state. It never imitates a physical clipboard or a fake control panel.

## Direction

- Shell: warm white ground, charcoal type, emerald as the primary action and healthy-state color.
- Typography: Inter with tabular numerals for operational quantities.
- Density: 8px spacing rhythm, medium information density, generous section separation.
- Signature move: prediction and actual consumption share the same chart axis, with forecast shown as a distinct dashed emerald series so the planning decision remains connected to evidence.
- Surfaces: 14px radius, 1px cool-gray borders, soft offset shadows reserved for elevated panels.
- States: emerald for on-track, amber for attention, coral for waste risk, slate for neutral metadata.

## First surface

The dashboard opens with the planning pulse: four high-signal measures, a forecast-versus-actual chart, a waste trend chart, and the latest records. A persistent rail makes Dashboard, Meal records, Predictions, and Waste analytics easy to reach.
